Deterministic Formula

Purity & Lot Normalization

Pnorm = [ (Praw / Qunit) × 10.0 × (999.0 / Fineness) ] + Assaying_Adj

• Quotation Scaling: Scales price per quoted unit to a standard 10-gram denomination.

• Assay Multiplier: For 995.0 fineness, scales by (999 / 995) = 1.004020×. For 999.0 coin contracts, multiplier is 1.0×.

• Assaying Adjustment: Factors in minor assay cert or blister pack charges (₹0 - ₹15/10g).

Governance Protocol

3-Tier Signal Verification Gate

A raw spread discrepancy is not an automatic trade. Every signal must clear 3 quantitative filters.

Rule 1: Friction Hurdle Min. ₹80 / 10g

Gross spread must exceed round-trip friction. If transaction drag consumes >50% of the spread, the signal is Rejected.

Purpose: Protect capital from cost erosion.
Rule 2: Tender Buffer ≥ 5 Trading Days

Contracts within 5 days of delivery tender notice are disqualified to eliminate compulsory physical vault delivery liability or penalty charges.

Purpose: Zero physical delivery obligations.
Rule 3: Range Sanity 1.5σ ≤ |Z| ≤ 3.0σ

Spreads under 1.5σ are deemed noise (Inconclusive). Spreads over 3.0σ indicate structural regime breaks (Rejected).

Purpose: Differentiate noise from edge.
